# Model Selection

This is an operational recommendation, not an absolute rule. Prefer a smaller, well-scoped session over increasing reasoning indiscriminately. Do not encode prices, quotas, or limits.

Use GPT-5.6 Terra Medium for mechanical implementation, documentation, simple tests, skills, clear refactors, and low-to-medium-risk apply work.

Use GPT-5.6 Sol Medium for architecture, complex `/opsx:propose`, persistence, concurrency, authentication, realtime work, difficult debugging, and decisions with many dependencies.

Use GPT-5.6 Sol High only after Medium cannot resolve a genuinely complex problem. Do not recommend High by default.
